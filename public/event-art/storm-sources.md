# Storm material sources

- Kenney, **Smoke Particles**, downloaded 2026-10-03 from https://kenney.nl/assets/smoke-particles
- CC0, personal and commercial use allowed; original notice: `kenney-smoke-license.txt`.
- `storm-puff-00.png`: original `PNG/White puff/whitePuff00.png`.
- `storm-puff-05.png`: original `PNG/White puff/whitePuff05.png`.
- `storm-fire.png`: original `PNG/Explosion/explosion00.png`.
- Kenney, **Particle Pack**, downloaded 2026-10-03 from https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack (CC0; original notice: `kenney-particle-license.txt`).
- `storm-cloud-01.png`, `storm-cloud-03.png`: original `PNG (Transparent)/smoke_01.png`, `smoke_03.png`; textured smoke replaces the smooth cartoon puffs in lightning/hail warnings.
- `storm-vortex.png`: original `PNG (Transparent)/twirl_02.png`; independently rotating projected cross-sections, combined with smoke textures. This is runtime compositing, not a purchased flipbook.
- Assets copied without modification. They are composited and animated at runtime; no external image requests are made by the widget.
- The existing `survival-tsunami-v2.png` water atlas is retained, with runtime depth-dependent deformation and independent foam particles. It is generated artwork, **not** an internet-source tsunami animation.

Research rejected: Alenia's pixel-art tornado (style/resolution mismatch); DELUGE demo (not a purchased production asset). Neither is incorporated into the site.
