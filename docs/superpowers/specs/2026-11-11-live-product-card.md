# Shared live product card

Approved: one product selection controls the PRISM/YouTube widget and the site card. OFF hides both; pause holds the current item; selected/history/all and manual pin use existing controls. No separate site settings. Rotation remains five seconds with a shared clock. Streaming latency may delay the burned-in YouTube widget relative to the site.

Site: one compact card below chat, above composer; thumbnail/name/price and “상품 선택”. No extra browse/count row on the live screen (user refinement). Multiple featured products: entire card opens the featured panel; one product: opens that exact product directly. Panel contains actual selected details, persistent full catalog escape and count. Bottom catalog button always opens all products, clearing search/filter. Options retain their selected product when the card rotates. No per-product public chat messages from widget-selection commands; private audit and intro registration stay. Normal customer questions and purchase notices stay.

Use existing catalog/settings/events; no infrastructure or schema change. Voice automation remains on hold. Validate hidden/deleted/soldout, empty selection, broadcast changes, pause/resume, mobile widths, full catalog return, and unchanged intro/cart behavior.

Responsive refinement: layout follows available width, not device names; live view splits at 640px. Short-height view reduces auxiliary greeting/heading and card image. Verify narrow folded/portrait, unfolded square, landscape, desktop and keyboard-sized viewports. Do not claim every physical device was tested.
