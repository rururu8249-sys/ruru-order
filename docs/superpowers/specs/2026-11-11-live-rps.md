Winners frozen at start, exact identities, rewards idempotent. 1–3 prominent winner names; 4+ two-column scroll, own result outside scroll; broadcast pages 10 names/5 seconds; admin table all records and reward status. Sound selected soft arcade voice, default on with mute and browser gesture unlock. No raw private identity/choices in public snapshot.
